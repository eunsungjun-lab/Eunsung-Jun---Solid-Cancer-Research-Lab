About

Researcher Profile

Eunsung Jeon is a cancer researcher studying mechanisms that drive therapeutic resistance in difficult-to-treat solid tumors. His research integrates tumor biology, the tumor microenvironment, spatial biology, organoid models, and immunotherapy to identify actionable therapeutic targets.

Research

Research Focus

Pancreatic Cancer

Mechanisms of tumor progression, therapeutic resistance, and molecular heterogeneity in PDAC.

Tumor Microenvironment

CAF, ECM, immune-cell infiltration, vascular components, and spatial interactions within the TME.

Precision Immunotherapy

Development and preclinical evaluation of next-generation immunotherapeutic strategies for solid tumors.

Patient-Derived Models

PDOs and humanized/preclinical models for patient-specific therapeutic response and mechanism studies.

Spatial Biology

Spatial transcriptomics and multiplex imaging to resolve tumor–immune–stromal architecture.

KRAS Biology

Functional and immune microenvironmental differences associated with KRAS mutation subtypes.
